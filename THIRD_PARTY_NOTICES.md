# Third-party data notices

## Harvard Growth Lab Atlas of Economic Complexity

The Week 3 classroom files were retrieved from the public GraphQL API of the
Growth Lab at Harvard University's *Atlas of Economic Complexity*:

- <https://atlas.hks.harvard.edu/>
- <https://github.com/harvard-growth-lab/api-docs/blob/main/atlas.md>

Underlying goods data are sourced by the Atlas from UN Comtrade. The files in
this repository were retrieved on September 28, 2026, and transformed into a
fixed 2024 HS92 four-digit country-product matrix for classroom analysis. See
the lab's `data/manifest.json` for the transformations and checksums.

The Atlas FAQ encourages reuse and adaptation with appropriate citation. These
third-party data are not relicensed under this repository's MIT or CC BY 4.0
terms and remain subject to the source providers' applicable terms.

Recommended citation:

> Growth Lab at Harvard University. *The Atlas of Economic Complexity*.
> Harvard Kennedy School. <https://atlas.hks.harvard.edu/>.
