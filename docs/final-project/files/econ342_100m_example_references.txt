These references reproduce the source memo's bibliography and data notes. The source memo and its references were accessed October 21, 2026. No source data or estimates have been updated for this teaching adaptation.

Source notes from the earlier memo

During the table-download outage, official publications were accessed through preserved retrieval-service text captures, not complete CSV cubes; CMHC HTML files were downloaded directly. Raw JSON captures are unchanged retrieval responses, not original PDFs. The ZIP records exact source locations, transformations, hashes, assumptions, and exclusions. Synthetic 2025 regional estimates hold 2021 shares constant; they are not observed CMA populations. Household size, vacancy, allocation shares, capacity ceiling, and policy thresholds are explicit assumptions, not official observations.
